# Example prompts & workflows

Things you can ask an assistant connected to the Planoly MCP server. These assume a workspace with connected channels — the assistant will call `list_workspaces` / `list_channels` on its own to orient itself.

## Posting & scheduling

> Draft an Instagram post from the latest photo in my media library with a caption about our summer sale, and schedule it for Friday at 10am.

> Cross-post my most recent draft to TikTok and Pinterest too — keep the caption on TikTok but shorten it for Pinterest and pin it to my "Products" board.

> Reschedule everything I have this week to next week, same weekdays and times.

> Schedule my "beach carousel" draft at the next Best Time slot.

> Find a trending TikTok pop sound and attach it to my scheduled Friday post.

> Put a trending Instagram sound on my reel draft and pin it to the "Spring" board on Pinterest too.

> Why didn't my post go out yesterday?

## Media library

> Import these four Canva export links into a new "October launch" folder in my media library.

> Show me what's in my media library folder "UGC" — preview the first few so I can pick one.

## Analytics & research

> How did my Instagram account do last month vs the month before? What content type drove the most interactions?

> Rank my last 20 published posts by saves and tell me what the top 3 have in common.

> Look at @competitorhandle's recent posts — what formats and hooks are they using, and how does their engagement compare to mine?

> Create a shareable report link of my post performance for the client.

> Compare my TikTok, LinkedIn, and Pinterest results for the last 30 days. Where am I growing fastest?

## Brand voice

> Learn my brand voice from my Instagram and website.

> Write a LinkedIn caption for this photo in my brand voice.

## Inbox

> What's waiting for me in my inbox? Summarize the unread DMs.

> Reply to the comments asking about pricing with my "Pricing" saved reply — show me each one before sending.

> Label every conversation mentioning "wholesale" as Leads and assign them to Sam.

## Automations

> Set up an automation on my latest reel: anyone who comments "GUIDE" gets a DM with the download link.

> Which of my automations got the most opens? Pause the ones that sent nothing this month.

## Help & billing

> How do I connect a Facebook page to Planoly?

> When does my plan renew, and how many posts do I have left this month?

## End-to-end

> Plan next week's content: analyze my top-performing posts from the last 60 days, draft 5 posts in a similar style using media from my library, and schedule them at Best Time slots. Show me the plan before scheduling.

## Tips

- The assistant can only post to channels that are connected in Planoly — connect channels in the app first.
- Reminder-only channels (e.g. a personal Instagram profile) get a notification to post manually instead of auto-publishing.
- Some tools (media library, analytics, trending sounds, product tagging, inbox) require a paid Planoly workspace; the assistant will surface an upgrade link if a tool needs one.
- Replies and automations reach real people on Instagram. The assistant is told to confirm the exact words and recipients with you before it sends or turns anything on.
